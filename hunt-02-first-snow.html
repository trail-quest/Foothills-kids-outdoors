🧡 Safety: It's hunting season in the foothills. Wear bright colours (blaze orange is great) and stay on marked trails. Never walk on early ice: AHS recommends at least 15 cm before walking or skating, and river ice is never safe to trust. Dress warm in layers, with a toque and mitts and spare socks. Always stay with an adult. Give deer, birds and all wildlife lots of space (bears are still awake until late November). Never taste berries or snow off plants.
