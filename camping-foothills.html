Foothills camping FAQ

Which Foothills campgrounds have power?
Riverbend in Okotoks (power, water and sewer, 30/50 A), Chain Lakes Provincial Park (15/30 A on 57 sites), Diamond Valley Municipal in Turner Valley (30 A on some sites) and George Lane in High River (powered sites; amps not listed). Archie & Janet Hogg Park has no power or water hookups. Power at Tales & Trails (Longview) and Bob Lochhead (Black Diamond) isn't listed on their official pages, so call ahead.
Where can we camp with kids near Longview?
Tales & Trails Campground is in the Village of Longview, which also has a playground and skate park, and it's open May 1 to the end of Thanksgiving weekend. Chain Lakes Provincial Park, on Hwy 22 south of Longview, has a lake, a pier and a playground at the day use area. Archie & Janet Hogg Park sits on the Highwood River between High River and Longview and has no power or water hookups.
Which ones take reservations?
Six of the seven take reservations. Booking at Tales & Trails (Longview) isn't listed, so call the Village. Chain Lakes books through Alberta Parks up to 90 days before arrival; the window moves forward each day at 9:00 a.m. MT. Archie & Janet Hogg Park is reservation only, through Let's Camp. Diamond Valley Municipal and Bob Lochhead book through the Foothills Lions Club (online availability for Diamond Valley; a request form or campground@foothillslionsclub.ca for Bob Lochhead). Riverbend books online, with a 2-night weekend minimum (3 on long weekends). George Lane books by phone (403-652-2529) or email, with a $5 reservation fee.
Which campgrounds have showers?
Riverbend has hot coin showers ($1 for 5 minutes) and Diamond Valley Municipal has one shower. Chain Lakes lists no showers. Showers at George Lane, Tales & Trails, Bob Lochhead and Archie & Janet Hogg Park aren't listed, so check before you go.
Can we bring the dog camping?
Riverbend allows leashed pets ($2 a day) and George Lane allows up to three dogs ($2 per dog per day). For the other campgrounds, pet rules aren't posted, so ask before you book. Keep dogs leashed and under control in bear country.
Are campfires allowed right now?
It changes, sometimes daily. Check the official Alberta fire ban map before you leave and again when you arrive, and only have a fire when conditions allow. Bring a camp stove in case fires are banned. At Chain Lakes, fires are only at the communal firepits; sites have no individual firepits.
