🎣 Highwood River: Kids' Fishing Summary

Cast your line, wait for the bite, and let most trout swim free!

Share: Facebook Email

📅 Season

Most of the Highwood is open June 16 to October 31. Seasons differ by stretch: the last 500 m before the Bow River is open all year, and Storm Creek is closed all year.

🚫 Bait Ban

No bait on any part of the Highwood. Use unscented lures or flies only (no scented lures or PowerBait).

🐟 Trout

Most trout must be released. In the upper headwaters you may keep up to 2 brook trout. Mountain whitefish can be kept on some stretches at some times, so check the limits.

🧒 Licence

Kids under 16 do not need a fishing licence, but they still follow the rules. Anglers 16 to 64 need a sportfishing licence. Alberta residents 65 and over don't (official licensing page).

Check before you go: Always check the official 2026 Alberta Guide to Sport Fishing Regulations before fishing. The Highwood River rules are in the official Eastern Slopes (ES1) rivers table. This page is a kid-friendly summary, not legal advice.

🗺️ Family fishing spots

Regulations change, so always check the official regs before you go. Last checked against the 2026 Alberta Guide to Sport Fishing Regulations on October 14, 2026. 🎒 What to bring

  • 🧥 Layers: foothills weather flips fast, so pack a warm layer and a rain shell.
  • 💧 Water for everyone, plus 🍎 snacks to keep little legs going.
  • ☀️ Sun or snow gear by season: sunscreen, hats and bug spray in summer; a toque, mitts and spare socks in winter.
  • 🩹 A small first-aid kit and any meds your kids need.
  • 🐻 Bear spray on Kananaskis and backcountry trails (and know how to use it).
  • 🗑️ A bag to pack out garbage, and a charged phone.
