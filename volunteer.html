🌿 Volunteer with your kids

Giving back outdoors is one of the best family adventures. Around the Alberta foothills, families can plant trees, pick up litter along the river, count winter birds and help care for trails. Kids learn to look after the places they love, and everyone gets fresh air together.

Dates change, so always confirm with the organizer. Ages, dates and sign-up rules come from each organizer's own page and can change from year to year. Use the links on each card to check before you go.

🎄 Light Up Longview

The Light Up Longview committee is setting up the village's Christmas lights in the campground and around the village, and invites all villagers to help. A hot lunch is served as a thank-you. Meet at the Longview campground cookhouse.

  • Organizer: Light Up Longview committee (Village of Longview)
  • Ages: Families welcome; kids with a parent.
  • When: Sunday, October 18, 2026, 10 AM to 2 PM.

🐎 Millarville Racing & Agricultural Society volunteers

Help with setup, admissions, parking, hospitality and vendor help at the Christmas Market, Farmers' Market, Canada Day Races and the Priddis & Millarville Fair. Free to volunteer, and volunteers get free admission to the society's events. Contact the office through the volunteer page.

  • Organizer: Millarville Racing & Agricultural Society
  • Ages: Volunteers range "from students to seniors." No minimum age given, check with organizer.
  • When: Christmas Market Nov 5 to 8 and Nov 12 to 15, 2026. The Summer Market runs Saturdays in summer and the Fair is in August.

Spring 2027

🛣️ Alberta Highway Cleanup

A yearly roadside litter pickup on provincial highways, including around Longview, Turner Valley and Black Diamond. Families can't sign up on their own; they join through a participating non-profit group, such as Junior Forest Wardens. Everyone must complete safety training first. Groups earn money for their organization.

  • Organizer: Alberta Transportation and Economic Corridors (through registered non-profit groups)
  • Ages: 9 and older. 1 adult for every 2 kids under 14, and 1 adult for every 5 kids aged 14 to 18.
  • When: First or second Saturday in May each year (2026 was May 2).

Alberta Highway Cleanup page

🌱 Okotoks Community Clean Up and Tree Planting

A spring cleanup of the Sheep River valley plus tree planting. There's a cleanup BINGO card with prizes and a free volunteer BBQ. Tree planters bring shovels, gloves, closed-toe shoes and eye protection.

  • Organizer: Town of Okotoks (with Parks & Recreation)
  • Ages: Open to individuals and families. No minimum age given, check with organizer.
  • When: Every spring. The 2026 event was Saturday, May 9; the 2027 date isn't posted yet.

🗑️ Okotoks Litter-Free Community cleanup kits

Pick up a free kit (gloves, bags and a rec coupon) and do a family litter pick in a park or along a pathway whenever it suits you. Register your cleanup and the town collects the bags. Stay back from busy roads and never pick up needles or glass.

  • Organizer: Town of Okotoks
  • Ages: Check with organizer.
  • When: Year-round.

Town of Okotoks Litter-Free page

🏞️ Calgary Pathway and River Cleanup

A citywide cleanup of pathways and riverbanks that has run every year since 1967, and was started by a 12-year-old! Registration is required.

  • Organizer: City of Calgary (Calgary Parks)
  • Ages: 12 and older; 12 to 17-year-olds must come with a parent or guardian. Not for kids under 12.
  • When: Every year in early May (2026 was May 1 to 3). Registration opens early in the year.

City of Calgary registration page

🐦 Sheep River Christmas Bird Count

A one-day winter bird census around Okotoks, Turner Valley and Black Diamond. Walk a route with a team, or count birds at your backyard feeder. All skill levels welcome, from the youngest birder up.

  • Organizer: Local count circle, Christmas Bird Count (Birds Canada)
  • Ages: Check with organizer.
  • When: Count season is December 14, 2026 to January 5, 2027. The 2026 circle date isn't posted yet (last year's was Dec 28).

🥾 Friends of Kananaskis Country: Volunteer Trail Care

Trail care days on Kananaskis trails, from litter pickup to clearing deadfall. Set up a volunteer profile first to get the list of upcoming days. Trail work can be physical, so ask whether a day suits kids.

  • Organizer: Friends of Kananaskis Country, with Alberta Parks
  • Ages: Check with organizer.
  • When: Days are posted through the season by email and on their calendar.
